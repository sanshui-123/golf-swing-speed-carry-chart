## Driver Swing Speed → Realistic Carry & Total Distance (Sea Level)

| Driver Swing Speed | Head Speed (m/s) | Typical Ball Speed | Realistic Carry | Typical Total | Common Shaft Flex |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **75 mph** | 33.5 m/s | ~110 mph | 160–172 yd | 178–192 yd | Ladies / Senior (A) |
| **85 mph** | 38.0 m/s | ~125 mph | 190–203 yd | 210–225 yd | Senior (A) / Regular |
| **95 mph** | 42.5 m/s | ~140 mph | 218–230 yd | 238–252 yd | Regular / Stiff |

> **Why golfers overestimate carry:** Many players quote their longest downwind summer total roll-out instead of average carry. Use carry distance to clear bunkers and water hazards.
